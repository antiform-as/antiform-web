# Antiform logo: shortlist

Kept 2026-10-08 from ten ideas for an icon mark. Not yet drawn beyond the favicon.

1. **The hand-drawn A, as is.** Now the favicon (`favicon.svg`): a pencil A traced as two
   strokes, `#8A554B` on paper `#DAD7D0`. One person's hand, from a company that exists
   because generated images are crowding out drawn ones.
2. **∀, the A upside down.** Logic's "for all": the simplest "anti" of the letter, and the
   platform favours nobody. Very legible at 16 px.
3. **A and ∀ meeting.** The letter and its inverted twin joined into one glyph, a diamond or
   an hourglass: form and its opposite.

Next step: sketch all three at full size and at tab size, side by side, before choosing.
