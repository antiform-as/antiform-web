Vipps for organisations on the Antiform platform

How to apply for Vipps products, what gets configured and by whom, and what to expect day to day.

This guide is written for your organisation's administrators — not developers. No technical integration work is required of you. Antiform is a registered Vipps Platform Partner and does the integration, development and maintenance.

Two points worth stating plainly:

  • Antiform never holds or moves your money. Funds flow directly from Vipps to your organisation's bank account. Antiform initiates payments against your sales unit; it is never a party to the settlement.
  • Antiform has no role in KYC or AML. Vipps verifies your organisation directly. If Vipps needs anything further, they contact you, not us.

Each product is ordered separately, and the order you apply in matters.

Login is not a sales unit of its own — it is switched on for a sales unit you already have. A sales unit ordered specifically for Login is permanently Login-only: Vipps cannot add a payment solution to it later, and it cannot be converted. Since you need Recurring for membership fees as well as Login for sign-in, order the payment (ecom) sales unit first, then activate Login on it.

Done in that order you end up with one sales unit, one Merchant Serial Number, and one set of API keys covering both products. Done backwards you end up with two of each, and the Login-only unit has to be replaced.

2. How the solution is configured

Almost all configuration is Antiform's job. This section exists so you know what is being set on your behalf, and which two items need a decision from you.

What you decide

  • Sales unit company name and logo. These appear on the Vipps login screen and in the Vipps app under "Companies with access". Members see this, so it should be the name they recognise your organisation by — not a legal entity name they will not place. Vipps requires both to be set before approving the Login product.
  • Terms and privacy URLs, if you collect consents.

The name and logo are set on your sales unit, by you, on the business portal — they are yours, not Antiform's. Members signing in see your organisation, never ours.

Every integration is exercised against the Vipps test environment first, using the Vipps Merchant Test (MT) app. Nothing touches real money until the production sales unit is live and you have confirmed the flows.

3. Using it day to day

Members logging in. A member clicks "Logg inn" on your site, chooses Vipps, confirms in the Vipps app, and returns signed in. There are no passwords for you to administer and no password resets to field. If a member cancels in the Vipps app, they land on a plain page explaining that login was cancelled, with a way to try again.

Membership fees. A member approves a Vipps mandate once. Recurring charges are then raised against that mandate on schedule. Members can see and stop the mandate from the Vipps app at any time — that is Vipps' guarantee to them, not something Antiform can override. Every charge attempt carries an idempotency key, so a network retry cannot double-charge a member.

When a charge fails — insufficient funds, an expired mandate, a member cancellation — the platform records the failure against the membership, notifies you as the tenant operator, and flags the membership for follow-up. After a configurable number of consecutive failures the membership is suspended rather than charged indefinitely. Deciding that threshold, and who chases the member, is yours.

When a member cancels. Cancellation is at period end. The Vipps agreement stops immediately, but the membership stays active and visible until the end of the period already paid for; a daily sweep then marks it lapsed. A member who cancels before their mandate was ever activated is cleared immediately, since there is no paid period to honour. The same rule applies when Vipps cancels the agreement from their side.

Reconciliation and invoicing. Vipps settles money directly to your bank account. Antiform records every successful charge and, at month end, invoices you for Antiform's share of the membership fees collected in that period — based on successful charges only. That invoice is Antiform to your organisation; it is not something your members see.

Member receipts. Each successful charge appears in the member's own Vipps app, labelled with the product name and description sent at mandate and charge time. The platform does not issue separate PDF receipts or formal invoices to members.

What Antiform can see. Enough to run the integration: which member holds which mandate, and the state and amount of charges. Antiform does not see card numbers, bank credentials, or anything Vipps holds about a member beyond the identity claims the login requests — name, phone number and email.

Getting help

Report Vipps-related problems to Antiform first. Antiform is your first line and escalates to Vipps MobilePay where needed; contacting Vipps directly usually routes back to us and costs a round trip.
